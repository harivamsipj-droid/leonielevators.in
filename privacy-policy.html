2. Information We Collect

a) Information you give us directly

  • Name, phone number, email address, and city/location when you request a quote via our website form, WhatsApp, or phone call
  • Property details relevant to your enquiry (location, number of floors/stops, building type) shared during a free site survey or quotation process
  • Any additional details you share in WhatsApp conversations or email correspondence with our team
  • Payment and billing details collected during the order process, handled per the payment terms in your signed quotation

b) Information collected automatically

  • Standard web analytics data (pages visited, time on site, device/browser type, approximate location via IP) via Google Analytics / Google Ads conversion tracking
  • Conversion events when you click our WhatsApp or Call buttons, or submit a form, tracked via Google Ads (Conversion ID: AW-18411596450) so we can measure which ads and pages lead to genuine enquiries
  • Cookies used by these third-party tools — see Section 5

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation for your property
  • To schedule and conduct a free site survey
  • To communicate with you about your order, installation timeline, and AMC/service visits
  • To send you relevant offers or updates, where you have not opted out
  • To measure and improve our Google Ads campaigns and website performance
  • To comply with legal, tax, and contractual obligations related to your purchase

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Who We Share Information With

  • WhatsApp / Meta: when you message us via our WhatsApp Business number, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to ours
  • Google (Ads & Analytics): for conversion tracking and site analytics, as described in Section 5
  • Installation and logistics partners: where necessary to deliver and install your elevator, or to carry out AMC visits
  • Payment processors: for handling advance and milestone payments as per your quotation
  • Legal/regulatory authorities: where required by law

We do not share your information with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies from Google Ads and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. This includes tracking when a visitor clicks our WhatsApp button, call button, or submits an enquiry form, so we know which ads are driving genuine leads. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some site functionality.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and customer information for as long as needed to fulfil your order, provide warranty and AMC service (your product carries a 2-year service warranty as standard, per your quotation), and meet our legal and tax record-keeping obligations. Analytics data is retained per Google's standard retention settings.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us at help@leonielevators.in to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to our legal and contractual obligations (for example, we may need to retain order and warranty records)
  • Opt out of promotional communications at any time

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults purchasing or enquiring about home elevators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
