What to Check Before Buying a Home Lift in Chennai

Before you sign with any brand — LEONI included — walk through this checklist:

  • Component quality. Ask what goes inside the lift. LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components; ask any vendor for the equivalent detail, not just a brochure claim.
  • Power-cut behaviour. Chennai summers bring load-shedding. Confirm the lift has an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup that lowers the cabin to the nearest landing and opens the doors.
  • Civil work required. Every home lift needs a standard lift pit and a proper shaft. A serious vendor surveys the site first and puts the civil scope and timeline in writing — LEONI manages this work end-to-end.
  • Coastal durability. For ECR, Neelankarai and Besant Nagar homes, ask how the finish and components handle salt-laden air. Corrosion-resistant finishes and quality components matter here.
  • Service reach. Choose a brand with PAN-India delivery and support, so a transfer to Coimbatore or Kochi never strands your investment.
  • Honest pricing. Indicative Indian market ranges suggest roughly ₹8–15 lakh for LEONI hydraulic lifts and ₹12–25 lakh for LEONI gearless MRL traction models — market estimates vary with floors, finish and technology. Treat any figure as a starting point and get a written, site-specific itemised quote.

Does a home lift need civil work in a Chennai home?

Yes — every home lift needs a standard lift pit and shaft, and we plan for it honestly. Our team manages the civil work end-to-end and shares the exact timeline after a free site survey; most Chennai installations take a few weeks from survey to handover.

Yes. Every LEONI lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If the power fails mid-ride, the cabin descends safely to the nearest landing and the doors open — you are never stuck between floors.
