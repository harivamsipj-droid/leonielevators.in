Which Bangalore Homes Suit a Pitless Lift?

The short answer: almost any home with two or more floors. The most common LEONI installations in Bangalore fall into three archetypes:

  • Independent villas and row houses — the G+1 and G+2 villas typical of Whitefield and Sarjapur Road have generous stairwell voids or double-height foyers where a self-supporting glass shaft becomes an architectural centrepiece rather than an afterthought.
  • Duplex and triplex apartments — penthouse duplexes need a compact footprint and silent operation. LEONI’s Machine-Room-Less (MRL) gearless drive runs whisper-quiet, with no rooftop machine room to negotiate with your builder or association.
  • Retrofits in older homes — houses in Koramangala or Indiranagar built decades ago were never designed for an elevator. Because a LEONI lift needs no pit and only minimal headroom, it can be added to a finished home without breaking the floor slab or touching the foundation.

If your home is still on the drawing board, even better: your architect can reserve a lift position early and treat the cabin finish—Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, Signature Glass or Classic Walnut—as part of the interior palette.

What Bangalore Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

A home lift is a decade-plus commitment, so evaluate any brand—including us—on these five points:

  • Safety certification. Insist on internationally audited equipment. LEONI lifts are TUV SUD certified.
  • Power-cut protection. Bangalore’s outages are real. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup should bring the cabin to the nearest floor and open the doors automatically—never accept a lift without it.
  • Civil work required. A pit or machine room means weeks of construction and structural risk in a finished home. Pitless, machine-room-less designs avoid both.
  • Service and AMC support. Ask how service visits are scheduled, what the annual maintenance contract covers, and whether spares are available PAN-India.
  • Cabin size and finish. Confirm the cabin comfortably fits your family—and a wheelchair if accessibility is a goal—and that finishes match your interiors rather than looking like a commercial lift.

On budget: market estimates suggest premium home lifts in India typically range from around ₹12 lakh for compact configurations to roughly ₹35 lakh for larger, highly customised cabins. Frequently Asked Questions

Do LEONI home lifts work during Bangalore power cuts?

Yes. Every LEONI lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If the power fails mid-ride, the cabin glides to the nearest floor and the doors open automatically, so you are never stranded between levels.

How long does installation take in an existing Bangalore home?

Because LEONI lifts are pitless and machine-room-less, a typical installation takes around 48 hours with no major civil work. There is no excavation, no structural alteration and no weeks-long construction site inside your home.

How much space does a pitless home lift need?

Far less than a conventional elevator. LEONI's self-supporting structure needs no pit and no machine room, so a compact corner, an atrium or the void beside a staircase is usually enough. Our team confirms the exact footprint during a free site survey.
