A Ludhiana Buyer’s Checklist Before Choosing a Home Lift

Before you compare brands on price alone, put these five points on paper:

  • Component quality. Ask what goes inside the lift — LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components.
  • Power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup should come standard for Punjab’s summers.
  • Service and AMC. Ask who services the lift, how fast, and what the annual maintenance contract covers.
  • Cabin size. A joint family needs a cabin that comfortably carries elders — check the usable dimensions, not just the shaft size.
  • Civil scope. Insist the quotation itemises the standard lift pit, the civil work and the exact timeline in writing.

On budget: as indicative market ranges, LEONI hydraulic home lifts are typically around ₹8–15 lakh and gearless MRL traction lifts around ₹12–25 lakh, depending on floors and finishes. Treat these as indicative figures — message LEONI on WhatsApp for an exact quote for your kothi.

Ludhiana Home Lift FAQs

Do LEONI home lifts work during Ludhiana's power cuts?

Yes. Every LEONI lift is fitted with an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) battery backup. During an outage the cabin automatically moves to the nearest floor and opens its doors, so no one is ever stuck inside.

Can a lift be added to an existing kothi without damaging it?

Yes, with proper planning. LEONI surveys the kothi first, then prepares a standard lift pit and manages the civil work around your flooring and structure. The exact timeline is shared upfront — typically a few weeks depending on the home.

How much space does a LEONI home lift occupy?

Only a compact footprint. Gearless machine-room-less (MRL) models need no separate machine room, so the lift can fit into a stair void, a duplex corner, or an external glass shaft. Final cabin and pit dimensions are confirmed during a free on-site survey.
