What Ahmedabad Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Before you sign any order form — with LEONI or anyone else — verify these five essentials:

  • Component quality. Ask what goes inside the lift. LEONI’s lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components — ask any vendor to detail their component sourcing.
  • Power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup is non-negotiable in Indian conditions; it should bring the cabin to the nearest floor and open the doors automatically.
  • Service and AMC support. Confirm response times and annual maintenance terms in writing before purchase, not after.
  • Cabin size and finish options. Measure your available footprint and match it against real cabin dimensions — and see finishes like Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, Signature Glass or Classic Walnut in person, not just in a brochure.
  • Installation plan. A professional installer surveys your site first, then shares an itemised quote and a realistic timeline covering the standard lift pit and civil work. If a vendor promises an overnight installation, ask why.

Ahmedabad Home Lift FAQs

Will a LEONI home lift work during an Ahmedabad power cut?

Yes. Every LEONI lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If the power fails mid-journey, the cabin glides to the nearest floor and the doors open automatically, so no one is ever stranded between floors.

How long does home lift installation take in Ahmedabad?

Timelines are shared upfront after a free site survey. Installation includes preparing a standard lift pit and the associated civil work, which our team manages end to end — typically a few weeks depending on the home.

How much space does a LEONI home lift need?

Less than many owners expect. Gearless machine-room-less (MRL) models need no separate machine room, so the lift can occupy a stairwell void, a corner of the living area, or a compact external shaft. A free site survey confirms the ideal cabin size and pit dimensions for your floor plan.
