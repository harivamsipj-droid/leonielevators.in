What Pune Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Compare every brand—including LEONI—on these five essentials before you commit:

  • Safety certification. Demand internationally audited equipment. LEONI lifts are TUV SUD certified.
  • Automatic Rescue Device (ARD). A battery-backed ARD that brings the cabin to the nearest floor during an outage is essential—monsoon-season power dips are a fact of life in Pune.
  • Civil work required. A pit and machine room add weeks of construction and structural risk. Pitless, machine-room-less designs install in about 48 hours with no major civil work.
  • AMC and after-sales service. Ask what the annual maintenance contract covers, how service visits are scheduled and whether spares are available PAN-India.
  • Cabin size and finish. Verify the cabin comfortably carries your family—and a wheelchair if needed—and that finishes feel residential, not commercial.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a LEONI lift keep working during Pune power cuts?

Yes. Every LEONI lift is fitted with an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) backed by a battery. If the power drops mid-journey, the cabin automatically settles at the nearest floor and opens its doors, so no one is ever stuck inside.

How much space does a home lift need in a Pune villa?

Much less than most owners expect. Because LEONI lifts are pitless and machine-room-less, the self-supporting shaft fits into a stairwell void, an atrium or a spare corner. A free site survey confirms the exact footprint for your home.

How disruptive is the installation of a pitless lift?

Minimal. There is no excavation, no machine room construction and no major civil work, so a typical LEONI installation completes in about 48 hours. You can continue living in your home while the lift goes in.
