What Chandigarh Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Before signing with any brand — LEONI included — insist on five things in writing:

  • Component quality. Ask what goes inside the lift — LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components.
  • Power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup should be standard, not an add-on.
  • Service and AMC. Get the maintenance commitment and response process documented before purchase.
  • Cabin size. Confirm the cabin comfortably fits the family members who will use it most — especially elders.
  • Civil scope. Every home lift needs a standard pit — a good quotation itemises the pit, the civil work and the timeline, so verify all three on paper.

On budget: as indicative market ranges, LEONI hydraulic home lifts are typically around ₹8–15 lakh and gearless MRL traction lifts around ₹12–25 lakh, depending on floors and cabin finishes. These are indicative ranges only — request an exact LEONI quote for your villa on WhatsApp.

Finally, think beyond the delivery date. A home lift is a long-term investment, and the years after installation matter more than the week of it. Prefer a brand that schedules preventive maintenance, stocks genuine spares, and answers its helpline. Chandigarh Home Lift FAQs

Do LEONI home lifts in Chandigarh work during power cuts?

Yes. Every LEONI lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If the power fails mid-ride, the cabin glides to the nearest floor and the doors open automatically, so you are never stranded between floors.

How long does a home lift installation take in Chandigarh?

Timelines are shared upfront after a free site survey. Installation includes preparing a standard lift pit and the associated civil work, which our team manages end to end — typically a few weeks depending on the villa.

How much space does a home lift need?

Far less than most owners expect. Gearless machine-room-less (MRL) models need no separate machine room, so only a compact footprint is required — a stairwell void, a corner of the foyer, or an external shaft often suffices. Exact cabin and pit dimensions are confirmed during a free site survey.
