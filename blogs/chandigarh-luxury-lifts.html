What Chandigarh Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Before signing with any brand — LEONI included — insist on five things in writing:

  • Safety certification. Ask for independent certification — LEONI lifts are TUV SUD certified.
  • Power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup should be standard, not an add-on.
  • Service and AMC. Get the maintenance commitment and response process documented before purchase.
  • Cabin size. Confirm the cabin comfortably fits the family members who will use it most — especially elders.
  • Civil scope. A true pitless design needs no excavation and no machine room — verify this on the quotation itself.

On budget: market estimates suggest premium home lifts in India typically range from about ₹12 lakh to ₹35 lakh depending on technology, number of floors, and cabin finishes. These are indicative market ranges only — request an exact LEONI quote for your villa on WhatsApp.

Finally, think beyond the delivery date. A home lift is a long-term investment, and the years after installation matter more than the week of it. Prefer a brand that schedules preventive maintenance, stocks genuine spares, and answers its helpline. Chandigarh Home Lift FAQs

Do LEONI home lifts in Chandigarh work during power cuts?

Yes. Every LEONI lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If the power fails mid-ride, the cabin glides to the nearest floor and the doors open automatically, so you are never stranded between floors.

How long does a home lift installation take in Chandigarh?

A typical LEONI installation is completed in about 48 hours. Because the lift is pitless and Machine-Room-Less (MRL), there is no excavation and no major civil work — your villa stays clean and livable throughout.

How much space does a pitless home lift need?

Far less than most owners expect. With zero pit and no machine room, a LEONI lift needs only a compact footprint — a stairwell void, a corner of the foyer, or an external shaft often suffices. Exact dimensions are confirmed during a free site survey.
