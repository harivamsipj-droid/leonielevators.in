The Technology Behind the Ride

Vacuum / Pneumatic (Air-Driven)

A pneumatic lift moves the cabin using controlled air pressure inside a sealed cylinder. No pit, no machine room, no counterweights — and a striking, sculptural look. Nibav built its brand on this technology; LEONI's Series V refines it with a quieter, more stable ride tuned for luxury interiors. Vacuum lifts typically suit one to two passengers and compact footprints.

MRL Gearless (Machine-Room-Less)

MRL gearless lifts use a compact permanent-magnet motor mounted inside the shaft itself, eliminating the rooftop machine room entirely. The result is a whisper-quiet, energy-efficient ride with generous cabin sizes — the technology behind LEONI's flagship pitless lifts and the reason we can offer full-size cabins in Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, Signature Glass and Classic Walnut.

Hydraulic

Hydraulic lifts push the cabin up with a fluid-driven piston. They are proven and smooth, but usually need a pit, a machine cabinet and periodic oil maintenance. Several home lift models in the Indian market — including some imported platforms — use hydraulic or screw-driven variants.

Traditional Traction

The classic rope-and-counterweight system that powers most commercial buildings. Excellent for high-rise duty cycles, but in a private home it typically demands a deep pit, headroom, a machine room and weeks of civil work — which is why residential-first brands have moved away from it.

Pit, Machine Room & Civil Work: The Retrofit Question

This is the single biggest divider. If your home is already built — marble laid, interiors done — digging a 1.5-metre pit through your foundation is disruptive, expensive and sometimes structurally impossible. Pitless designs from LEONI and Nibav sidestep the problem entirely; conventional designs typically cannot. Our complete guide to pitless home lifts explains exactly how a zero-pit installation works and what your builder needs to know.

Installation Time: Days vs Weeks

A conventional lift project is a construction project: excavation, shaft masonry, scaffolding, wiring, inspections. Expect it to typically run several weeks. LEONI's pitless systems arrive as engineered modules and are commissioned in roughly 48 hours — your home stays a home, not a site. Vacuum lifts from other brands are also typically quick; imported platforms tend to sit in between, depending on shipping and site readiness.

An honest word here: Johnson Lifts' service network is a genuine strength — scale matters when you have thousands of commercial units in the field. LEONI's model is residential-only — our Hyderabad HQ coordinates PAN-India support, and because our lifts have no pit machinery or machine room, there is simply less to maintain.

Customisation: A Lift That Matches Your Interiors

A home lift is furniture as much as machinery. Nibav's transparent capsule is a design statement in itself, and imported catalogues offer tasteful European palettes. Safety: ARD and Certification

Two things are non-negotiable in an Indian home lift. First, an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) — battery backup that brings the cabin to the nearest floor and opens the door during a power cut, standard on every LEONI lift. Second, independent certification: LEONI's systems are TUV SUD certified. Whichever brand you choose, ask for the certificate, not the brochure claim — and ask what happens mid-ride when the power fails, because in India it will.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which home lift is best if my house has no pit?

Pitless designs are the answer. LEONI's MRL gearless and Series V pneumatic lifts need zero pit and no machine room, so they can be installed in finished homes without breaking the floor. Vacuum lifts from brands like Nibav are also pitless, while conventional traction or hydraulic lifts typically require pit excavation.

How long does home lift installation take in India?

It depends on the technology. Conventional lifts that need a pit, shaft work and a machine room typically take several weeks including civil work. LEONI's pitless plug-and-play systems are installed in around 48 hours with no major civil work.

What happens to a home lift during a power cut?

Every LEONI lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) — a battery backup that lowers the cabin to the nearest floor and opens the door automatically during a power failure. Given how common outages are in Indian cities, we consider ARD non-negotiable; always confirm this feature with any brand you evaluate.
