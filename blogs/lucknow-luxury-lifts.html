What Lucknow Buyers Should Verify Before Choosing a Home Lift

Elegance matters, but diligence matters more. Confirm these five points with any supplier:

  • Component quality. Ask what goes inside the lift — LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components.
  • Power-cut protection and door safety. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup is non-negotiable in Uttar Pradesh, and infra-red door sensors should guard the entrance as standard.
  • Service and AMC. Understand the annual maintenance contract and who attends breakdowns, in writing.
  • Cabin size. Verify the cabin suits daily use by elders — comfort at 7 AM matters more than the brochure photo.
  • Civil scope. Every home lift needs a standard pit — get the pit, civil work and timeline itemised on the quotation.

Lucknow Home Lift FAQs

Do LEONI home lifts in Lucknow run during power cuts?

Yes. Each lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup that lowers the cabin to the nearest floor and opens the doors automatically during an outage — essential peace of mind for elders using the lift alone.

How quickly can a home lift be installed in a Lucknow bungalow?

Timelines are shared upfront after a free site survey. Installation includes preparing a standard lift pit and the associated civil work, which our team manages end to end — typically a few weeks, whether it's a classic Hazratganj bungalow or a new Gomti Nagar villa.

Is a pit or machine room required for the lift?

A standard lift pit is required, and our team manages that civil work for you. A machine room depends on the model: gearless machine-room-less (MRL) traction lifts need no separate machine room, while geared traction models use one. A free site survey confirms the right fit and exact dimensions.
