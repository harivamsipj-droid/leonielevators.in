A Kolkata Buyer’s Checklist for Choosing a Home Lift

Whatever brand you shortlist, verify these five essentials before paying an advance:

  • Component quality. Ask what actually goes inside the lift — LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components.
  • Power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup should be included as standard — it is on every LEONI lift.
  • Service and AMC. Confirm the annual maintenance terms and breakdown response commitment in writing.
  • Cabin size. Measure for the people who will actually ride daily — elders, and any mobility aids they use.
  • Civil scope. Every home lift needs a standard lift pit and a proper shaft — insist the civil scope and timeline appear on the quotation. LEONI manages this work end-to-end.

Kolkata Home Lift FAQs

Do LEONI home lifts work during Kolkata power cuts?

Yes. Every LEONI lift carries an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If power fails, the cabin settles at the nearest floor and the doors open automatically — no one is ever trapped inside.

Can a home lift be added to a heritage house in Kolkata?

Yes, and it is a real civil project. Installation includes preparing a standard lift pit and shaft; our team manages the civil work with the care a heritage property demands and confirms the exact scope and timeline after a free site survey — typically a few weeks depending on the home.

How much space does the lift need in a compact Kolkata home?

Less than most owners expect. A LEONI lift needs a compact shaft and a standard lift pit, and our gearless traction range offers machine-room-less (MRL) options that free up roof space. Exact dimensions and pit depth are confirmed during a free site survey.
