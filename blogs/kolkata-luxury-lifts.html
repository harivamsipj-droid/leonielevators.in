A Kolkata Buyer’s Checklist for Choosing a Home Lift

Whatever brand you shortlist, verify these five essentials before paying an advance:

  • Safety certification. Ask for independent third-party certification — LEONI lifts are TUV SUD certified.
  • Power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup should be included as standard.
  • Service and AMC. Confirm the annual maintenance terms and breakdown response commitment in writing.
  • Cabin size. Measure for the people who will actually ride daily — elders, and any mobility aids they use.
  • Civil scope. A true pitless lift needs zero excavation and no machine room — insist this appears on the quotation.

On budget: market estimates suggest premium home lifts in India typically range from about ₹12 lakh to ₹35 lakh depending on technology, floors, and cabin finishes. Treat these as indicative Indian market ranges — message LEONI on WhatsApp for an exact quote for your home.

A final word on longevity: Kolkata’s humidity and long monsoon months are unforgiving on machinery, so ask every supplier how the drive system and cabin finishes are protected and how often preventive maintenance visits happen. Kolkata Home Lift FAQs

Do LEONI home lifts work during Kolkata power cuts?

Yes. Every LEONI lift carries an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If power fails, the cabin settles at the nearest floor and the doors open automatically — no one is ever trapped inside.

Can a home lift be installed in a heritage Kolkata house without civil work?

Yes. LEONI's pitless design needs zero excavation and no machine room, which is why it suits Alipore and Ballygunge heritage homes. Installation takes about 48 hours with no major civil work.

How much space does the lift need in a compact Kolkata home?

Very little. With no pit and no machine room, a LEONI lift fits into a stair void, a corner of the living area, or an external shaft. Exact dimensions are confirmed during a free site survey.
