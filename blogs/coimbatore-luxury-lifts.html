What Coimbatore Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Coimbatore's engineering-minded buyers ask sharp questions — here is the checklist we recommend for any brand, ours included:

  • Safety certification. Demand independently audited certification. LEONI's lifts are TUV SUD certified.
  • Power-cut behaviour. Confirm the lift ships with an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) and battery backup that lands the cabin at the nearest floor and opens the doors.
  • Civil work. A true pitless lift needs no excavation and no machine room. If the vendor's drawings show a pit, you are looking at older technology.
  • Ride quality and noise. Gearless MRL drives run remarkably quietly — ask for a demonstration ride before you commit.
  • Service and AMC. Ask about response times in Tamil Nadu and what the annual maintenance contract includes. LEONI backs Coimbatore clients with 24/7 support.
  • Cabin size and finish. Measure for wheelchair access if needed, and compare finishes — Signature Glass, Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, and Classic Walnut.

Coimbatore Home Lift FAQs

How long does home lift installation take in Coimbatore?

A typical LEONI installation takes about 48 hours after the site survey. Because the lift is pitless and machine-room-less, there is no excavation and no major civil work — even in an occupied family home.

How much space does a pitless lift need in a Coimbatore bungalow?

Much less than a conventional elevator. With zero pit and no machine room, the lift can occupy a stairwell void, a corner of the hall, or run along an external wall. LEONI engineers confirm the exact footprint during a free site survey.

What happens to the lift during a power cut?

Every LEONI lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If the power fails mid-ride, the cabin travels to the nearest floor and the doors open automatically, so nobody is ever stuck inside.
