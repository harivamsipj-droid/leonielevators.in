What Coimbatore Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Coimbatore's engineering-minded buyers ask sharp questions — here is the checklist we recommend for any brand, ours included:

  • Component quality. Ask what goes inside the lift. LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components.
  • Power-cut behaviour. Confirm the lift ships with an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) and battery backup that lands the cabin at the nearest floor and opens the doors.
  • Civil work. Every home lift needs a standard lift pit and a proper shaft. A serious vendor surveys your home first and puts the civil scope and timeline in writing — LEONI manages this end-to-end.
  • Ride quality and noise. Gearless MRL drives run remarkably quietly — ask for a demonstration ride before you commit.
  • Service and AMC. Ask about response times in Tamil Nadu and what the annual maintenance contract includes. LEONI backs Coimbatore clients with 24/7 support.
  • Cabin size and finish. Measure for wheelchair access if needed, and compare finishes — Signature Glass, Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, and Classic Walnut.
