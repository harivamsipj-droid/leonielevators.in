Which Indore Homes Suit a Pitless Lift?

The beauty of a zero-pit, machine-room-less design is how many housing formats it fits:

  • New-build villas — G+1 to G+3 homes in Vijay Nagar and Bicholi Mardana can reserve a compact shaft in the stairwell or lobby at the planning stage, with no special foundation work.
  • Duplex residences — a two-stop cabin links the living and private floors of a duplex without eating into carpet area, ideal for multi-generational households.
  • Established bungalows — older homes in Saket, Old Palasia and Manoramaganj retrofit beautifully, because the self-supporting structure rests on the existing slab with no digging or demolition.

What Indore Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Whichever brand you shortlist, put these five points on your checklist first:

  • Independent safety certification. LEONI lifts are TUV SUD certified; ask every vendor for equivalent third-party audit documents.
  • ARD battery backup. In Indian power conditions, an Automatic Rescue Device that lands the cabin at the nearest floor and opens the doors is essential, not optional.
  • AMC and after-sales service. Get maintenance frequency, response times and AMC pricing in writing before you pay an advance.
  • Cabin dimensions and finishes. Verify that the quoted cabin genuinely fits your stairwell or corner — and inspect finishes like Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, Signature Glass and Classic Walnut physically.
  • True pitless installation. A genuine pitless MRL or vacuum lift installs in about 48 hours with no major civil work. Extensive masonry quotes are a red flag.

Indore Home Lift FAQs

Do I need to dig a pit or build a machine room for a home lift in Indore?

No. LEONI's pitless, Machine-Room-Less (MRL) design needs zero excavation and no rooftop machine room, so your bungalow's foundation and flooring stay completely untouched.

What happens if the power fails while the lift is in use?

Every LEONI lift is fitted with an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD). Its battery backup brings the cabin to the nearest floor and opens the doors automatically, so a power cut never leaves your family stranded.

How quickly can a home lift be installed in Indore?

Around 48 hours once the lift arrives at your site. With no pit to dig and no major civil work involved, installation is clean, quiet and dust-free — well suited to India's cleanest city.
