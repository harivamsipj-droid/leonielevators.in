What Vizag Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

A home lift is a long-term investment — on the coast, doubly so. Before choosing any brand, verify:

  • Corrosion protection. Ask what materials and sealing the cabin and drive use. Coastal humidity punishes cut corners within a few monsoons.
  • Safety certification. Look for independently audited certification — LEONI's lifts are TUV SUD certified.
  • Power-cut behaviour. Confirm an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup is standard, not an optional extra.
  • Service reach. Ask how quickly a technician can reach Visakhapatnam and what the AMC covers. LEONI supports Vizag through its dedicated AP service network.
  • Cabin size and finish. Check wheelchair access if needed, and review finishes — Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, Signature Glass, and Classic Walnut.

Vizag Home Lift FAQs

Can a home lift handle Vizag's salty coastal air?

Yes — if it is built for it. LEONI's sealed pitless design and corrosion-resistant finishes such as Titanium Gold are chosen specifically for coastal conditions, and a regular maintenance schedule keeps the drive system protected through humid sea-facing seasons.

Will the lift work during a power cut in Visakhapatnam?

Yes. Every LEONI lift carries an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. If the supply fails mid-ride, the cabin moves to the nearest floor and the doors open automatically, so no one is ever stranded between levels.

About 48 hours once the site survey is done. Because LEONI lifts are pitless and machine-room-less, there is no excavation and no major civil work — the shaft rests on your existing floor slab.
