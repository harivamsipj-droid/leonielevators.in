The National Buyer's Checklist

Wherever you live, hold every home lift brand — LEONI included — to the same five standards before you sign:

  • Component quality. Ask what actually goes inside the lift. LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components, and use infra-red door sensors.
  • Power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup should be standard equipment in India, not a paid extra — it is fitted to every LEONI lift.
  • Service and AMC. Get the annual maintenance contract terms and breakdown response process documented before purchase.
  • Cabin size. Confirm usable cabin dimensions against the family members who will ride daily — especially elders.
  • Civil scope. Every traction or hydraulic lift needs a standard lift pit and a proper shaft — insist the civil scope and timeline are written into the quotation. LEONI manages this work end-to-end.

On budget: as indicative Indian market bands, LEONI hydraulic lifts typically sit around ₹8–15 lakh and LEONI gearless MRL traction lifts around ₹12–25 lakh, depending on floors served and cabin finishes; vacuum capsules from specialist vendors such as Nibav generally occupy a higher band again.
