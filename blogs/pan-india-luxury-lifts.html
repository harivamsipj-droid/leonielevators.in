Which Indian Homes Suit a Pitless Lift?

The honest answer: far more than most owners assume. Because a LEONI lift is pitless, Machine-Room-Less (MRL), and self-supported, it adapts to the three archetypes that dominate India's premium housing stock:

New Villas & Duplexes

Plan the shaft into the design from day one — a Signature Glass column in the atrium or a discreet corner install. No pit in the foundation plan, no machine room on the terrace.

Retrofits & Older Homes

Zero excavation means an existing bungalow or kothi gains a lift without touching its foundation. Most retrofits are running in about 48 hours, with no major civil work.

Multi-Generational Homes

Where elders share the home, a compact cabin with ARD battery backup turns every floor into safe, accessible living space — the single most common reason Indian families call us.

The National Buyer's Checklist

Wherever you live, hold every home lift brand — LEONI included — to the same five standards before you sign:

  • Safety certification. Insist on independent third-party certification. LEONI lifts are TUV SUD certified.
  • Power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup should be standard equipment in India, not a paid extra.
  • Service and AMC. Get the annual maintenance contract terms and breakdown response process documented before purchase.
  • Cabin size. Confirm usable cabin dimensions against the family members who will ride daily — especially elders.
  • Civil scope. A genuine pitless lift requires zero excavation and no machine room — verify this on the quotation itself.
