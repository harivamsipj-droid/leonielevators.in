What Surat Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

A home lift should serve your family for decades. Evaluate every brand—including LEONI—on these five points:

  • Component quality. Ask what goes inside the lift. LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components.
  • Automatic Rescue Device (ARD). A battery-backed ARD that lowers the cabin to the nearest floor during a power cut is essential—never buy a lift without one.
  • Civil work required. Every home lift needs a standard lift pit and a proper shaft. A serious vendor surveys the site first and puts the civil scope and timeline in writing — LEONI manages this end-to-end, and our gearless MRL options remove the machine room.
  • AMC and service network. Ask what the annual maintenance contract covers, how quickly technicians respond in Gujarat, and whether spares are available PAN-India.
  • Cabin size and finish. Confirm the cabin comfortably fits your family—including a wheelchair if accessibility matters—and that the finish options feel residential and premium.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can a home lift be installed in a Surat bungalow?

Installation includes preparing a standard lift pit, so the timeline depends on your home. Our team manages the civil work end-to-end and shares an exact schedule after a free site survey — typically a few weeks from survey to handover.

Do LEONI lifts need a pit or a machine room?

Every LEONI lift needs a standard lift pit, which our team prepares and manages as part of the installation. A machine room is optional: our gearless traction range offers machine-room-less (MRL) options, while geared traction models use a compact machine room. What happens if the power fails while the lift is in use?

Every LEONI lift includes an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup. During an outage the cabin automatically travels to the nearest floor and opens its doors, so passengers exit safely even in a blackout.
