What Hyderabad Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Before you sign with any brand — LEONI included — run through this checklist:

  • Component quality. Ask what goes inside the lift. LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components.
  • Power-cut behaviour. Hyderabad summers bring outages. Confirm the lift has an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) with battery backup that brings the cabin to the nearest floor and opens the doors — standard on every LEONI lift.
  • Civil work. Every home lift needs a standard lift pit and a proper shaft. A serious vendor surveys your home first, plans the civil work, and gives you an itemised quote — LEONI manages this end-to-end.
  • Service and AMC. Ask who maintains the lift, how quickly they respond, and what the annual maintenance contract covers. LEONI operates from its Hyderabad HQ with round-the-clock support.
  • Cabin size and finish. Measure for wheelchair access if needed, and view finishes — Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, Signature Glass, and Classic Walnut — before choosing.
