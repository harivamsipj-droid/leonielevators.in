What Delhi NCR Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

The capital has no shortage of elevator vendors — filter them with these five checks:

  • Component quality. LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components; ask every brand you compare to detail their component sourcing.
  • ARD battery backup and door safety. An Automatic Rescue Device that lands the cabin at the nearest floor and opens the doors during an outage should be standard equipment, not an optional extra — and so should infra-red door sensors.
  • AMC terms and service reach. Confirm written response times and annual maintenance costs for your specific sector or colony before paying an advance.
  • Cabin size, capacity and finish. Match quoted cabin dimensions to your real floor plan, and inspect finishes — Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, Signature Glass, Classic Walnut — in person.
  • Installation plan. A serious installer surveys your site, then commits an itemised quote covering the standard lift pit, civil work and a realistic timeline in writing — treat instant-install promises with caution.
