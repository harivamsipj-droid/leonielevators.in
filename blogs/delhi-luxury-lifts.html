What Delhi NCR Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

The capital has no shortage of elevator vendors — filter them with these five checks:

  • Independent safety certification. LEONI lifts are TUV SUD certified; demand equivalent third-party audit paperwork from every brand you compare.
  • ARD battery backup. An Automatic Rescue Device that lands the cabin at the nearest floor and opens the doors during an outage should be standard equipment, not an optional extra.
  • AMC terms and service reach. Confirm written response times and annual maintenance costs for your specific sector or colony before paying an advance.
  • Cabin size, capacity and finish. Match quoted cabin dimensions to your real floor plan, and inspect finishes — Titanium Gold, Royal Marble, Signature Glass, Classic Walnut — in person.
  • Installation footprint. A genuine pitless lift needs no excavation, no machine room and about 48 hours on site. Long civil-work schedules signal older technology.
