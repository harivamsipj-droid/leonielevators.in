What Jaipur Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Before committing to any brand, run through this five-point checklist:

  • Component quality. LEONI lifts are built with BIS/ISI-marked and CE-marked components; ask any vendor you evaluate to detail their component sourcing.
  • ARD power-cut protection and door safety. An Automatic Rescue Device with battery backup should bring the cabin to the nearest floor and open the doors automatically, and infra-red door sensors should guard the entrance — verify both are standard, not paid add-ons.
  • Service network and AMC. Ask for written maintenance schedules, response commitments and AMC costs before paying an advance.
  • Cabin size and finishes. Confirm real cabin dimensions against your available space, and view finishes like Royal Marble, Titanium Gold, Signature Glass and Classic Walnut in person.
  • Transparent installation plan. Expect a site survey first, then an itemised quote covering the standard lift pit, civil work and a realistic timeline — instant-install promises deserve hard questions.
