Because LEONI needs no pit and no machine room, nearly every Pink City housing format qualifies:

What Jaipur Buyers Should Check Before Choosing a Home Lift

Before committing to any brand, run through this five-point checklist:

  • Third-party safety certification. LEONI lifts are TUV SUD certified; insist on comparable independent audits from any vendor you evaluate.
  • ARD power-cut protection. An Automatic Rescue Device with battery backup should bring the cabin to the nearest floor and open the doors automatically — verify this is standard, not a paid add-on.
  • Service network and AMC. Ask for written maintenance schedules, response commitments and AMC costs before paying an advance.
  • Cabin size and finishes. Confirm real cabin dimensions against your available space, and view finishes like Royal Marble, Titanium Gold, Signature Glass and Classic Walnut in person.
  • Genuine pitless installation. A true pitless lift installs in about 48 hours with no major civil work — heavy masonry quotations deserve hard questions.

Jaipur Home Lift FAQs

Can a home lift be installed in an older Jaipur bungalow without damaging it?

Yes. LEONI's pitless design requires no excavation and no machine room, so heritage-style homes in areas like C-Scheme can be retrofitted without touching the original foundation or stonework.

Will the lift keep working during a power cut?

Yes. The built-in Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) uses battery backup to bring the cabin to the nearest floor and open the doors automatically whenever mains power fails, so nobody is left stranded.

How long does home lift installation take in Jaipur?

Approximately 48 hours once the lift is delivered to your site. Because there is no pit to dig and no major civil work to schedule, your home stays clean and liveable throughout the installation.
